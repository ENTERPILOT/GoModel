## Summary

GoModel is released under the permissive MIT license. We want to keep it open
and practical for developers, small teams, and independent projects.

A small commercial distribution, [GoModel Pro](/pro/overview), adds prompt
compression, intelligent routing, OIDC SSO, and a few other extensions on top
of the same open-source gateway. Those extensions are licensed separately, not
MIT.

## Why open source

An AI gateway is infrastructure. It sits between every request your application
makes and every model it talks to. That kind of component should be something
you can read, audit, extend, and trust. This is why GoModel is open source.

## Sustainability

Building GoModel is how we make a living. That is why we offer a commercial
distribution — [GoModel Pro](/pro/overview), with prompt compression,
intelligent routing, OIDC SSO, and a few other extensions. The gateway is
MIT-licensed and fully usable without a license; Pro targets companies that
profit directly from the project, not individual developers or small teams
building with it.

If a Pro feature is one you need and paying for Pro is not an option for you,
tell us — a cheaper personal-use license, or open-sourcing that feature, is
negotiable. See the [FAQ](/about/faq) for the longer answer.
